# AI news digest: 2026-09-11 to 2026-09-16

Compiled from 66 emails across 24 senders. Items 1 to 16 of
`ai_news_2026-09-11_to_2026-09-14.md` still hold; this file
adds the follow-ups and the news from Sept 14 to 16.

## 1. Dario Amodei: "We Must Pace the Frontier"

Anthropic's CEO asked labs to slow capability gains so safety
work can catch up. His plan has three steps: embedded
third-party evaluators such as METR, shared standards among
democratic labs (which he says needs an antitrust waiver), and
talks with authoritarian governments. Altman, Musk, Hassabis
and Nadella agreed within two days. The AI Evaluator Forum
published AEF-1, a baseline for independent evaluations.
SK Hynix and Kioxia fell 6.4% on Monday; the Philadelphia chip
index fell nearly 6%.

Covered by 14 newsletters.
Sources: [darioamodei.com](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier)

## 2. Trump, Beijing and Jensen Huang reject the slowdown

Trump called AI doom a "HOAX" and said a "High IQ" president is
the only guardrail needed. China's Foreign Ministry called the
plan fear-mongering. At Dreamforce Jensen Huang said "We don't
need any new laws." OpenAI's Chris Lehane said OpenAI,
Anthropic and Google DeepMind have coordinated on safety for
weeks and need no antitrust waiver. David Sacks and Ben
Thompson criticized the proposal; Cohere's Aidan Gomez warned
it could entrench the largest labs.

Covered by 7 newsletters.
Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/14/trump-ai-safety-anthropic-dario-amodei), [Cohere](https://cohere.com/blog/who-gets-to-define-the-rules-for-ai)

## 3. Apple ships Siri AI in iOS 27

English-only beta with personal context across Messages, Mail
and Photos, on-screen awareness and in-app actions. Apple says
its new foundation models were built with Google's Gemini.
Not in the EU or China at first. Private frameworks show
Claude or ChatGPT could later replace Siri's model.

Covered by 6 newsletters.
Sources: [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/14/siri-can-be-swapped-out-for-chatgpt-claude)

## 4. TypeSafe launches Jev, a "System One" model

Diogo Almeida, who worked on the research behind ChatGPT, left
stealth with a model that only decides, classifies, routes and
scores. It picks from preset options, so it cannot produce free
text. TypeSafe claims 20 to 200x faster and 40 to 400x cheaper
than LLMs, $42 per billion input tokens and free output.

Covered by 5 newsletters.
Sources: [TypeSafe](https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev)

## 5. Periodic Labs Neon beats GPT-6 Astra on lab data analysis

Built on Kimi K2.6 (1T parameters), trained on 1,300 H200s with
RL on real lab data. FrontierXRD success rose from 2.7% to
55.3%, above Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 at lower cost. It runs
in Periodic's labs in the search for superconductors and
magnets.

Covered by 2 newsletters.
Sources: [Periodic Labs](https://periodic.com/news/nature-is-our-learning-environment)

## 6. Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.5 Transcribe

Real-time voice models with background tool calls. The
Extended Thinking version keeps talking while it reasons and
tops Artificial Analysis' speech-to-speech board. 97+
languages. $0.005/min input and $0.018/min output audio.
Newsletters quoted other prices; these are from Google's post.

Covered by 5 newsletters.
Sources: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/build-real-time-voice-applications-gemini-audio/)

## 7. Salesforce Koa and Salesforce in Claude

Koa is Salesforce's first CRM reasoning model, post-trained
from NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super on synthetic data. Salesforce
claims 3x fewer errors on its own CRM benchmark and hosts the
weights itself. Separately, Salesforce in Claude entered beta
with 37 sales skills.

Covered by 5 newsletters.
Sources: [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/09/15/koa-reasoning-model/), [Claude](https://claude.com/blog/salesforce-in-claude)
