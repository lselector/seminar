# AI news digest: 2026-09-14 to 2026-09-21

Compiled from 81 newsletter emails across 24 senders.

## 1. Pacing debate: Amodei's slowdown essay and the backlash

Dario Amodei proposed three layers of "pacing": embedded third-party evaluators, coordination among labs in democracies, and global coordination. Altman and Musk backed it; Trump called AI doom a hoax, Beijing called it fear-mongering, and Jensen Huang and Zuckerberg declined. Anthropic named Accenture's Faculty as its first embedded evaluator, with both sides expecting to invest at least $1B over five years. Four subscribers filed an antitrust class action over the slowdown. Newsom ordered work on a frontier-model kill switch, and the US proposed an AI incident notification mechanism to China.

Covered by 14 newsletters.
Sources: [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/ai-leaders-want-to-hit-the-brakes-after-years-of-reckless-speed/), [Tom Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/what-does-the-pause-mean/)

## 2. TypeSafe launches Jev, a non-generative decision model

Ex-OpenAI researcher Diogo Almeida's TypeSafe released Jev, which returns typed decisions with confidence scores instead of text. It costs $0.042 per 1M input tokens, output is free, and responses take 70 to 500 ms. Vercel says it reached about 13% of AI Gateway teams in a day. Six open clones appeared within two days.

Covered by 9 newsletters.
Sources: [TypeSafe blog](https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev)
