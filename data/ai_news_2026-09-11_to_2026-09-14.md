# AI news digest: 2026-09-11 to 2026-09-14

Compiled from 40 emails across 22 senders. The mail folder
held only these four days, so the window is short.

## 1. Dario Amodei: "We Must Pace the Frontier"

Anthropic's CEO published an essay on Saturday asking labs to
slow capability gains so safety work can catch up. He says AI
is now speeding up its own development and that agent swarms
could take over large parts of the internet within 6 to 12
months. His plan: embedded third-party evaluators with the
right to publish, shared standards among democratic labs
(which needs an antitrust waiver), and talks with authoritarian
governments. Altman, Musk, Hassabis and Nadella agreed within
two days. Trump called slowdown advocates "negative forces."
SK Hynix and Kioxia fell 6.4% on Monday, ASML 4.4%, SoftBank
up to 13%.

Covered by 8 newsletters.
Sources: [darioamodei.com](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier)

## 3. DeepSeek V4.1-Flash replaces V4-Pro

A 552B MoE model (763B with auxiliary parts) with native vision
and 1M context. It uses 8B active parameters for input and 16B
for output through a new causal encoder-decoder design. KV cache
is 890 bytes per token, a quarter of V4-Flash. Reported 90.6 on
Terminal-Bench 2.1, MIT-licensed weights, 50% off-peak discount.
DeepSeek later said V4 Pro API access continues after Sept 14.

Covered by 6 newsletters.
Sources: [AINews](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-deepseek-v41-flash-763b-p8b)

## 4. OpenAI won't IPO in 2026; Anthropic heads to Nasdaq

Altman told Fortune an IPO this year would be ill-advised given
safety concerns; a 2027 listing is reportedly under
consideration. Anthropic told shareholders it expects a second
profitable quarter on $11.5B Q2 revenue and has picked Nasdaq
for an October listing, with Nvidia in talks to anchor up to
$10B. SoftBank closed an $11.9B loan from about 20 banks to
keep funding OpenAI.

Covered by 5 newsletters.
Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/12/openais-sam-altman-says-it-would-be-ill-advised-to-go-public-in-2026/), [Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/09/14/companies/softbank-loan-openai/)
